01 · Why We Survey

Our Testing Purpose

💡

The Simple Truth

Our role is to test surveys on behalf of the client. The client provides quotas, and we check whether the survey works correctly before real respondents take it.

🔍

What We Check

Survey loading, completes (Status 6), terminates (Status 7), quota behavior and unexpected errors.

📋

What the Client Gives

Quota targets such as age, gender and location. Always follow the latest client requirements.

⚠️

Error Reporting

Broken links or incorrect behavior should be reported promptly through the approved error-reporting process.

02 · Workflow

Step-by-Step Survey Testing

1 · Client sends quota

Review the survey link and required target group.

2 · We test the survey

Use the required approved test environment and check expected outcomes.

3 · Error found?

Record the survey link, error type and observed behavior.

4 · Senior escalation

Senior team members review and escalate confirmed issues to the client.

5 · Survey confirmed

Once verified, the survey can be considered ready for launch.

03 · Basics

What Is a Survey?

📝 Definition

A structured method of collecting feedback, opinions or data from respondents about specific topics, products, services or experiences.

🎯 Purpose

Surveys help organizations make data-driven decisions by understanding what people think, feel and experience.

🧭 Who Takes Surveys?

Consumers, professionals, patients, caregivers and other people who fit the client's target criteria.

🔑 Your Role

You act as a survey tester, simulating a relevant respondent to verify that the survey behaves as expected.

04 · Key Terms

LOI, IR & Quota

⏱️ LOI — Length of Interview

Estimated time required to complete a survey. The training material specifies that timing starts from the main survey rather than the initial link click.

📊 IR — Incidence Rate

The percentage of people who qualify for a survey. Example: 1,000 start and 300 qualify → IR = 30%.

🎯 Quota

Pre-defined target groups such as age, gender or location. Always use the latest quota update.

05 · Essentials

Before You Start

🌐 Testing Environment

Use only the approved testing setup required by the project. Follow the client's current instructions.

🔄 Switcher

Match the required device/OS mode: Desktop, Mobile or Agnostic.

🕐 Timezone

Check and apply the correct timezone when the project requires it.

🗺️ ZIP & DMA

ZIP targeting may require a specific geographic test area. DMA refers to a specific media market/region.

06 · Status Codes

Understand the Outcome

6 · ✅ Complete

Survey finished successfully.

7 · ❌ Terminated

Respondent did not qualify or was screened out.

8 · 🔒 Quota Full

The target group has already reached its limit.

10 · ⚡ Security Termination

07 · Error Reporting

When Something Goes Wrong

⚠️ Report promptly

If a survey link does not load, behaves unexpectedly or produces an incorrect result, record the survey link, error type and what you observed, then use the approved error-reporting channel.

Capture

Link · Error type · Exact behavior · Relevant screenshot/details if required.

Escalate

Senior team members can review and communicate confirmed issues to the client.

08 · Question Types

OE vs CE Questions

✍️ Open-Ended (OE)

Free-text responses in your own words. Answers should be clear, relevant and genuine.

🔘 Closed-Ended (CE)

Predefined choices such as multiple-choice options or rating scales. Read all options before selecting.

Quality principle: respond truthfully and according to the actual study requirements; do not fabricate experiences or identities.
09 · B2C

Business to Consumer Surveys

These surveys gather consumer opinions about products or services, focusing on usage, satisfaction and consumer needs.

Classification

Age, gender, location, income and other demographic information.

Main Survey

Product feedback, usage frequency, comparisons and likelihood of recommendation.

Quality

Keep answers consistent and logical and read each question carefully.

OE

Give clear, relevant and genuine opinions when free-text is requested.

10 · B2B

Business to Business Surveys

B2B surveys are conducted with professionals and decision-makers within organizations for business strategy, product development or market research.

💬 Customer Feedback
📈 Market Research
🏷️ Brand Awareness
🔧 Product Development
👥 Employee Satisfaction
💻 Website/App Usability
11 · B2B Structure

Qualification → Main Survey → Classification

1 · Qualification

Screen by company size, industry, role and current product usage where required.

2 · Main Survey

Gather insights on satisfaction, industry trends and product improvement.

3 · Classification

Capture geography, revenue and decision-making information as required.

Key Difference

B2B respondents are professionals. Answers should reflect the relevant business context.

12 · Healthcare

Healthcare Survey Groups

🩺 HCPs

Doctors, nurses, administrators and assistants. Topics may include medicines, equipment, procedures and patient outcomes.

🤝 Patients & Caregivers

Personal experiences with medications or treatments, including effectiveness, side effects, quality of life and treatment satisfaction.

Important: healthcare research requires extra accuracy. Never fabricate medical information.
13 · Quality

Five Quality Check Points

1️⃣ Response Consistency

Answers should align logically.

2️⃣ No Speeding

Do not complete surveys unrealistically fast.

3️⃣ No Flat-Lining

Rating answers should reflect genuine consideration.

4️⃣ Device/Browser Compatibility

Match client requirements before starting.

5️⃣ QC Questions

Follow embedded instructions exactly.

14 · OE Guidelines

Writing Great Open-Ended Answers

✅ DO

  • Be clear, concise and relevant.
  • Give genuine opinions.
  • Vary sentence structure.
  • Add examples or suggestions when appropriate.

❌ DON'T

  • Leave answers blank or vague.
  • Use copied or fabricated responses.
  • Overuse punctuation or symbols.
  • Repeat identical wording across answers.
15 · Golden Rules

Remember These Principles

  1. Test according to the client's current requirements.
  2. Report survey errors through the approved channel.
  3. Respect LOI, IR and quotas.
  4. Keep answers consistent and logically possible.
  5. Read every question and instruction carefully.
  6. Give genuine, relevant open-ended answers.
  7. Never fabricate medical, professional or personal information.
16 · World Knowledge

Official vs Native Language

Official Language

A language legally recognized for government administration, courts or education, depending on the country.

Native Language

The language or languages commonly spoken natively by a population. It may differ from an official language.

The original Day 1 material includes regional examples across North America, South America, Europe, Asia, Africa, Oceania and the Middle East.
